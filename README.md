# module-1-benchmark-assignment
**Which countries' CO2 emissions are rising fastest, and which are falling?**
   Over the last thirty years or so, who is actually
   changing direction, not just who is biggest?
   
**Which industries contribute most to global emissions?**
   Not which countries - which *sectors*. Your manager has seen plenty of
   emissions-by-country charts and finds them unactionable.
   
   **Is Arctic sea ice actually declining, and by how much?**
   Your manager has heard the claim and the pushback and wants to see the record.
